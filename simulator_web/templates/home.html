Alliance Registry

Why register your alliance?

Registering gives your alliance a live, centralized database of every player — built from real, up-to-date information instead of scattered spreadsheets.

📋 How registration works

  • Sign in with Discord so every member is a verified, real player.
  • Create your alliance with its name, tag, kingdom and logo.
  • The creator automatically becomes the alliance admin.
  • Invite players directly, or let them request to join — the admin approves every request. Matching invites and requests are validated instantly.

🚀 What you get

  • Full control over your alliance's player database, always up to date.
  • Real data to plan strategies and formations instead of guesswork.
  • Every player's power, VIP, troop tiers and hero progression in one place.

🛠️ Tools we're building for registered alliances

  • Player filtering — search and segment members by power, VIP, troop tier or hero progression.
  • Hive (Colmena) builder — organize rally formations using real member data.
  • Bear Hunt trap wave planner — generate optimized waves from registered troop data.
  • Event planning tools — Swordland, Tri-Alliance and other coordinated event plans.

About this project

Built by one person, for the community

Behind kingshot.es there's no company or team — just KnyCat, a Spanish digital marketing agency owner who got into Kingshot and couldn't help but build tools for it.

Over 20 years connected to gaming communities, esports operations director, and someone who genuinely enjoys contributing knowledge and tools to the people they play with. The bot started small and keeps growing based on player feedback — this is a living project, shaped by the community that uses it.

Skills in web design, video editing, graphic design, programming and Discord setup all ended up in one place: a free bot and a growing web platform for Kingshot players.

This project is free — and self-funded

No ads, no subscriptions, no business intent. All server and infrastructure costs come out of pocket. If the project grows and costs become a real issue, that may need to change — but for now, it stays free.

If you find value in these tools and want to help keep them running, a small donation goes a long way.

Support via PayPal Share feedback on Discord

Ideas, bug reports and suggestions are just as valuable as donations. Every bit of feedback makes this better.
