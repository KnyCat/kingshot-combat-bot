Why register your alliance?

Registering gives your alliance a live, centralized database of every player — built from real, up-to-date information instead of scattered spreadsheets.

  • Sign in with Discord so every member is a verified, real player.
  • Create your alliance with its name, tag, kingdom and logo.
  • The creator automatically becomes the alliance admin.
  • Invite players directly, or let them request to join — matching invites and requests are validated instantly.
  • Full control over your alliance's player database, always up to date.
  • Real data to plan strategies and formations instead of guesswork.
  • Every player's power, VIP, troop tiers and hero progression in one place.
  • Player filtering — search and segment members by power, VIP, troop tier or hero progression.
  • Hive (Colmena) builder — organize rally formations using real member data.
  • Bear Hunt trap wave planner — generate optimized waves from registered troop data.
  • Event planning tools — Swordland, Tri-Alliance and other coordinated event plans.
