Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Summary

Kingshot.es does not collect, store, or process any personal data. There are no user accounts, no tracking cookies, and no analytics services.

Data collection

This website does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No registration or login is required to use any feature.
  • No names, email addresses, IP addresses or device identifiers are stored.
  • No form submissions are saved on our servers.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies of any kind — not for session management, analytics, advertising, or preferences.

Third-party services

Some links on this site lead to external platforms (Discord, PayPal, community wikis). These third parties operate under their own privacy policies. We have no control over the data they may collect when you visit them.

Hosting

This site is served from a self-hosted Raspberry Pi through Cloudflare Tunnel. Cloudflare may log standard connection metadata (e.g. IP address) as part of its network infrastructure. Refer to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out via Discord.
